Booking & Appointments

How do I book an appointment?
Fill out the form on our Book Appointment page with your details, preferred date and time, or call/WhatsApp us at 86880 33072. Our team will confirm your slot — usually within 30 minutes during clinic hours.
Will I receive confirmation of my appointment?
Yes. Once you submit a booking request, our team reviews it and contacts you by phone or WhatsApp to confirm the exact appointment time.
Is an initial consultation available?
Yes, an initial consultation with Dr. T. Venkat Rao or Dr. T. Sai Vakula is available for all new patients to assess your dental needs.
What are your clinic timings?
We are open every day of the week, including Sundays and public holidays, from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
Do you take walk-in patients, or is booking required?
Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking in advance through our website or by calling us so we can reserve a dedicated time slot for you.
Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?
Yes, simply call or WhatsApp us at 86880 33072 and our front desk will help you reschedule or cancel.
Is parking available at the clinic?
Yes, convenient parking is available near our clinic on TKR College Road, Karmanghat.

Treatments & Pain

What treatments do you provide?
We offer dental implants, root canal treatment, braces & aligners, teeth whitening, crowns & bridges, dentures, smile makeovers, wisdom tooth removal, pediatric dentistry, full mouth rehabilitation, and general checkups.
Is dental treatment painful at your clinic?
Most procedures, including root canal treatment, are performed with effective local anaesthesia and gentle technique. Our patients consistently describe their treatment as comfortable and painless.
Do you treat children?
Yes, we provide gentle pediatric dental care for children of all ages, including routine checkups and cavity treatment.
How experienced are your dentists?
Dr. T. Venkat Rao brings over 35 years of clinical experience and founded the clinic in 1989. Dr. T. Sai Vakula is an MDS-qualified specialist in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery with 11+ years of experience. Learn more about us →
Do you handle dental emergencies?
Yes, we accommodate dental emergencies such as severe toothache, swelling, or a broken tooth with same-day appointments wherever possible.

Before Your Visit

What should I bring to my first visit?
Please bring any previous dental records or X-rays if available, a list of current medications, and details of any medical conditions.

Pricing & Payment

How much does treatment cost?
Costs depend on your specific condition and treatment needed. We offer honest, transparent pricing with no hidden charges. Call 86880 33072 for a personalised estimate.
Do you offer payment plans or EMI options?
Yes, we offer flexible EMI and installment options to help make larger treatments like implants and full mouth rehabilitation more affordable.
