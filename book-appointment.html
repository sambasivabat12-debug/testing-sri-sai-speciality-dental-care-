Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment? +
Simply fill out the form above with your details, preferred date and time. Our team will call or message you to confirm your slot — usually within 30 minutes during clinic hours.
Will I receive confirmation? +
Yes. Once you submit the form, our team reviews your request and contacts you by phone or WhatsApp to confirm the exact appointment time.
Is consultation available? +
Yes, an initial consultation with Dr. T. Venkat Rao or Dr. T. Sai Vakula is available for all new patients to assess your dental needs.
What treatments do you provide? +
We offer dental implants, root canal treatment, braces & aligners, teeth whitening, smile makeovers, wisdom tooth removal, pediatric dentistry, full mouth rehabilitation, and general checkups.
What are your clinic timings? +
We are open every day of the week, including Sundays, from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. There is no day off — you can walk in or book any day that suits you.
Do you take walk-in patients, or is booking required? +
Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking in advance through this page or by calling us so we can reserve a dedicated time slot and minimize your waiting time.
Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment? +
Yes, simply call or WhatsApp us at 86880 33072 and our front desk will help you reschedule to a more convenient date and time.
Is the treatment painful? +
Most procedures, including root canals and extractions, are performed with effective local anesthesia and gentle technique. Our patients consistently describe their treatment as comfortable and painless.
Do you treat children? +
Yes, we provide gentle pediatric dental care for children of all ages, including routine checkups, cavity treatment, and guidance on healthy oral habits from an early age.
What should I bring to my first visit? +
Please bring any previous dental records or X-rays if available, a list of current medications, and details of any medical conditions. This helps our doctors plan your treatment safely.
Do you handle dental emergencies? +
Yes, we accommodate dental emergencies such as severe toothache, swelling, or a broken tooth with same-day appointments wherever possible. Call us directly at 86880 33072 for urgent concerns.
How experienced are your dentists? +
Dr. T. Venkat Rao brings over 35 years of clinical experience, and Dr. T. Sai Vakula is an MDS-qualified specialist in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, ensuring expert care across all treatments.
Is parking available at the clinic? +
Yes, convenient parking is available near our clinic on TKR College Road, Karmanghat, making it easy to visit by car or two-wheeler.
