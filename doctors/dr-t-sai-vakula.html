About

International Training, Advanced Surgical Care

Dr. T. Sai Vakula earned his Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery and further advanced his expertise through specialized training in Oral Implantology from Italy.

His professional journey includes serving as a Consultant Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon at leading healthcare institutions, where he gained extensive experience in implant dentistry, oral surgery, facial trauma management, and complex surgical procedures.

His commitment to excellence, innovation, and patient-centered care has earned him recognition among patients and peers alike.
