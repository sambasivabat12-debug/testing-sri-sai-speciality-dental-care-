Who We Are

A Legacy of Trusted Dental Excellence

Sri Sai Speciality Dental Care is a leading multi-speciality dental clinic in Karmanghat, Hyderabad, serving individuals and families with comprehensive dental solutions. Founded by Dr. T. Venkat Rao, our clinic has earned the trust of generations through ethical treatment, clinical excellence, and personalised patient care.

Today, our team combines decades of experience with modern dental technology to provide preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and surgical dental treatments under one roof — all delivered with the warmth and accountability of a practice that has always called this community home.
