Our Story

A Clinic Built On Trust

Sri Sai Speciality Dental Care was founded in 1989 by Dr. T. Venkat Rao on TKR College Road, Karmanghat — at a time when the area had very few dedicated dental practices. What began as a single dentist's commitment to honest, careful treatment has grown into a multi-speciality clinic trusted by thousands of families across Karmanghat, LB Nagar and greater Hyderabad.

Over three decades, Dr. Venkat Rao built a reputation for skilled, conservative dentistry — solving problems other clinics said could only be fixed with extraction. Today, he is joined by Dr. T. Sai Vakula, a fellowship-trained Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon and Implantologist, bringing advanced surgical and implant expertise under the same roof. Together, they continue the same founding promise: ethical advice, gentle treatment, and care that feels personal.
